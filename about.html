About Protective Security Group

Fifteen years of protecting what matters most.

Founded in 2009 as a sole trader and incorporated in 2013, Protective Security Group operates from Mayfair, London — specialising in high-value asset protection for the fashion, jewellery and luxury sectors.

SIA Accredited · Est. 2009
Our Story

The trusted authority in luxury retail security.

For over 15 years, we have served as the trusted authority in luxury retail security, delivering unmatched protection and sophistication to our valued clients. Every member of our team maintains an accredited SIA (Security Industry Authority) licence — a mandatory standard for private security work in the UK, and one we treat as a baseline, not a benchmark.

Our mission is to provide unparalleled security solutions through customer focus, expertise, and integrity. We are dedicated to safeguarding people, property, and assets by delivering tailored, reliable, and proactive security services.
